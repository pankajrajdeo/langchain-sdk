# How our GTM Agent builds consistent, high-quality sales artifacts

- Our GTM Agent builds sales documents that reps used to put together by hand for every deal, and they come out consistent and on brand.
- Most of the quality comes from the context we gave the agent rather than from prompt tuning. Because GTM Agent also knows where each deal stands, it can suggest the right document before a customer call.
- You can replicate this work on top of the Deep Agents harness, and Managed Deep Agents already includes the sandbox, skills, and memory it depends on.
- Deals where a rep sent one of these documents to the customer moved to a later stage 47% of the time, compared with 16% for matched deals without one.
