# Why human insight is a strategic advantage for building better agents

- Human expertise defines what good looks like. Domain experts bring the judgment needed to identify quality, explain why an interaction succeeds or fails, and uncover gaps in automated evaluations.
- Context is essential to evaluating agents. Reviewing the full conversation, including earlier messages and tool calls, helps teams understand not just what an agent answered, but how and why it got there.
- Human feedback becomes the foundation for better evals. Structured scores and reviewer comments can be used to calibrate LLM judges, improve online evaluators, and build golden datasets for continuous testing.
